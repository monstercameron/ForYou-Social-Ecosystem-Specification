# Sustainable economics and service contracts

## Scope and principles

Normative operational profile `operations-1.0`, paired with [security profile 1.0](./02-protocol.md). No compulsory treasury, network token, centralized price setter, exclusive payment intermediary, global feed, or reward for raw traffic is introduced. Each operator sets its own prices and contracts. Admission is permissionless to implement but acceptance depends on independently selected provider trust. Payment buys a bounded service, not an audience, universal acceptance, permanent storage, or legal immunity.

## Funding and immutable plans

`GET /v1/plans/{plan_id}` returns a signed immutable `LaunchPlan` with fields provider_id, plan_id, network, amount_base_units, credited_units, unit_costs, service_offer_id, signature. network and amount use the invoice encodings below. credited_units is a positive safe integer. unit_costs maps every baseline public message type to a positive safe integer, with no other keys. No conversion rate or rounding applies: one exactly paid invoice grants exactly credited_units, and each message consumes its type's integer cost. Unused credits have no expiry or recurring debit in profile 1.0. The purchased bounded service offer and its retention/read limits are defined in [Binding the offer to payment](#binding-the-offer-to-payment). plan_id is `fyp1:` plus lowercase SHA256 hex of P("plan-id", the plan excluding plan_id and signature). The signature covers P("plan", the plan excluding signature). The invoice's plan_id commits to these prices, network, amount and credit; provider and client MUST verify all matching fields. A changed schedule requires a new plan and explicit client consent. Use the LaunchPlan schema.

Invoice `expires_at` is compared to the timestamp of the canonical block that contains the qualifying transfer, not the time confirmations are reached. This is a protocol deadline convention, not a precise wall-clock guarantee. Exactly one qualifying transfer pays an invoice; partial payments are not aggregated. Duplicate exact payments enter the exception queue. Attribution and unit credit are atomic with consumption of the unique transfer. All amounts are satoshis for bitcoin-mainnet and wei for ethereum-mainnet.

Funding requires an authenticated pre-issued provider-signed invoice. Its fields are invoice_id, provider_id, author_id, plan_id, network (`bitcoin-mainnet` or `ethereum-mainnet`), amount_base_units (positive canonical decimal string), recipient, expires_at, and signature using label `invoice`. The provider assigns a fresh destination per invoice, persists its binding before returning it, never reassigns it, and credits only that invoice's author/plan. Anyone may pay an invoice; observing the transfer does not confer control of its credit. Launch supports BTC UTXO outputs and successful top-level native ETH value transfers only, not tokens, internal calls or contracts. Each BTC (network, txid, output_index) or ETH (network, tx_hash) is consumed once in the provider's durable ledger across all accounts/plans. Validate the actual network, recipient and exact expected base-unit amount from a trusted chain node; user-supplied amounts are hints. A paid invoice cannot be credited again. Under/overpayments and late transfers enter an exception queue without automatic credit; refunds require authenticated owner handling and never use a destination supplied by an unauthenticated claimant.

BTC credits after six confirmations; ETH credits only after the transfer is included in the node's finalized canonical chain. Pending deposits give no spendable units. On reorganization or invalidation, atomically reverse unspent credit, freeze new issuance when the account is negative, and reconcile without double-credit when the same transfer returns. The provider bears risk for already issued receipts; do not retroactively invalidate signed historical messages. A refund or withdrawal must atomically reserve its value against admission debits before initiating payment; retry with a stable settlement identifier and reconcile uncertain transactions before sending another. Clients must not label confirmation counts an unconditional guarantee.

## Binding the offer to payment

Every LaunchPlan MUST include service_offer_id. `GET /v1/service-offers/{service_offer_id}` returns a ServiceOffer signed by provider_id. Compute service_offer_id as `fyso1:` + lowercase SHA256 hex of P("service-offer-id", the object excluding service_offer_id and signature); sign P("service-offer", the object excluding signature), using [the protocol specification's](./02-protocol.md) byte rules. The plan hash commits to that ID. An invoice consequently commits to prices AND the service limits. A provider MUST serve the exact purchased offer while it has outstanding obligations; changing it creates a new ID and plan. Clients verify signatures, matching provider and hash before approval. No mutable URL alone defines paid terms.

The signed offer includes:

- max_message_bytes and max_content_bytes_per_message (positive safe integers, measured after decompression; all attachments count).
- retention_seconds: positive minimum retained duration per accepted message, starting at receipt issued_at; no infinity or discretionary early eviction for capacity reasons.
- read_bytes_per_message and discovery_requests_per_message: nonnegative lifetime budgets funded by that message. Zero explicitly means a separately funded read path, not free unlimited reads.
- replica_count: positive count of contracted copies, including the provider; replicas must disclose distinct controlling entities, correlated hosting and jurisdiction risks before claiming independence.
- availability_target_basis_points: integer 0–10000; success means a valid, complete authorized response within the disclosed latency limit, excluding neither operator faults nor ordinary demand within contract limits.
- export_grace_seconds: at least 2,592,000 (30 days) after scheduled service expiry or voluntary closure notice; additional lawful retention exceptions are governed by [the compliance specification](./04-compliance.md).
- terms_digest: SHA256 hex of the exact UTF-8 terms bytes; terms MUST specify remedies, taxes, refund policy, dispute venue, read-budget access controls, capacity thresholds and latency measurement.

Legal prohibitions can override storage or delivery, but require the decision/remedy process in [the compliance specification](./04-compliance.md). They do not authorize an undisclosed commercial downgrade. New purchases require affirmative approval of changed limits. No automatic credit charge for viral reads, retries, rejected messages, appeals, statutory rights requests, or ordinary account export. Default budgets are per admitted message and shared across readers, not repeated per visitor. Paid renewals require explicit approval and a separately signed order; they must not rewrite the original receipt or double-count its units. Read/retention contracts are service agreements, never evidence of public-message admission.

## Service lifecycle

Before payment, deliver the exact terms bytes with the offer over HTTPS; clients hash and archive them and refuse purchase if unavailable or mismatched. A dated prospective legal/privacy policy update may be necessary, but must preserve accrued consumer/service rights and historical evidence. An old terms digest is never authority for now-unlawful processing.

Before issuing a charged admission receipt, obtain and verify all declared referenced bytes and the complete attachment inventory, durably activate the storage and promised replica copies, and check capacity atomically with debit. `issued_at` is that activation time; the full retention period starts then. Storage/replica failure before activation returns 503 without debit. No pending receipt may consume paid retention time. Abandoned uploads can be garbage-collected under a separately disclosed staging limit. Availability failures after activation invoke the purchased remedies.

The pre-receipt copies above are the authorized intent and verified content bytes; a complete receipt-bearing, author-signed transport message does not yet exist. Replica acknowledgments first attest durable prepared intent/content with temporary reservations. Commit the receipt, debit and paid content-retention obligation only after those acknowledgments and final-size preflight. Do not claim finalized public-message replication at that point. The client then supplies the completed signed message through `POST /v1/messages` to its admitting provider, which verifies it, durably replicates the final metadata to the contracted replicas and marks the service finalized before returning 201 (200 for a completed retry). One idempotent finalization worker per message_id; partial replica failure returns 503 and the next retry resumes without new debit. Nodes must not expose prepared unsigned metadata as published messages. Public distribution to other independent hosts remains separate.

Retention of prepared content begins at receipt issued_at because that paid service has begun; client UI must distinguish `receipt_issued` from `finalized`. The provider accepts finalization without an extra admission charge throughout the paid retention window, using historical receipt-time validity rules. Client failure to finalize does not create a published post or extend the paid deadline; prepared content is retained for the full purchased period and export grace. Provider inability to finalize triggers the purchased service remedies, with no automatic second charge. After expiry, a valid signed historical message may still be accepted by another willing host, but it does not resurrect the expired provider's storage obligations. A lost finalization response is recovered by retrying the same signed message.

The included owner export entitlement includes an initial complete resumable export of all retained owner data, with three restart attempts, independently funded from public-read quotas. Subsequent snapshots use the incremental/full cadence below; new owner data does not reset the full-transfer allowance. Begin delivery within 24 hours and finish within seven days of an authenticated request; operators must cap sales/data volume so this is achievable. Security throttles cannot eliminate successful export or statutory rights. Provider-caused unavailability and failed integrity checks extend grace by their duration plus seven days after service restoration. Voluntary closure cannot shorten a purchased retention period without a lawful, owner-approved settlement; export ends no earlier than the scheduled retention end plus purchased grace, or the explicitly agreed lawful settlement deadline. Unforeseen insolvency cannot guarantee performance; preserve the independently tested backup path in [the portability specification](./05-portability.md).

Subsequent exports after new data may be incremental: deliver a complete newly signed manifest and all new/changed bytes relative to an owner-confirmed verified manifest, preserving unchanged local files and their hashes. Never delete a prior local archive until the merged replacement verifies. Include one fresh full export per 30-day period, the initial full export, and one additional full exit export at closure/grace regardless of that cadence, each with three resumable restarts. A user who loses local backups can use that full-export entitlement; statutory rights take precedence over these voluntary service limits. The cadence and incremental behavior are disclosed before purchase. Genuine additions do not reset the full-byte allowance on every write. Reserve/model these bounded complete transfers and all incremental newly changed bytes separately from public reads; do not charge another exit fee. Incremental transfer is not a complete portable bundle until every manifest entry has been materialized and verified at the client.

### Exact paid-renewal authorization

`POST /v1/renewal-quotes` accepts an authenticated owner request identifying provider_id, plan_id, target_message_id and added_seconds. The provider returns a root-provider-signed RenewalQuote: quote_id (32 random bytes in canonical base64url), provider_id, author_id, plan_id, service_offer_id, target_message_id, added_seconds (positive safe integer), units_charge (positive safe integer), expires_at and signature. Sign P("renewal-quote", quote without signature). The offer must belong to the provider; the target must be retained for this author. The offer and quoted additional period explicitly describe the extension; no other service limit is silently reduced. The user approves the exact charge and signs P("renewal-order", `{provider_id,author_id,quote_hash}`), where quote_hash is SHA256 hex of P("renewal-quote", quote without signature).

`POST /v1/renewals` carries `{quote, authorization}`. Verify provider quote and root-author signature (posting delegation is insufficient), owner/plan entitlement, offer and price, unexpired unused quote and available capacity. Atomically reserve extension obligations, debit exactly units_charge once, record quote_hash and extend retention from max(current paid end, server acceptance time) by added_seconds. Return 201 `{quote_hash,retention_until}`; a retry returns the original 200 result before checking expiry, with no extra debit or extension. A conflicting reused quote_id returns 409; failed checks/debit produce no extension. Do not charge or extend from an unauthenticated GET or copied quote without authorization. A provider using automatic renewal needs a future separately reviewed consent protocol; it is excluded here.

Receipt issuance atomically reserves the full contracted storage/replica obligation and read capacity budget as well as debiting units. Reject oversize or unfulfillable intents BEFORE debit. If a provider charges admission but subsequently fails to deliver the promised service, the remedy is a documented service credit or refund under the purchased terms, not removal of signed history. Clients retain receipts and terms as evidence.

Storage progresses through reserved, active, renewal_due, export_grace and expired; early legal restriction is a separate state. Notify the owner at least 30 days before discretionary expiry or planned closure (if a purchased duration is shorter, disclose the expiry at purchase and still provide export grace). No automatic public-access renewal fee. During grace preserve data for authenticated export where lawful, but public delivery can end at the purchased deadline. After grace, purge under the retention schedule unless independently renewed or lawfully held. Remaining copies held by others have their own contracts; do not imply global deletion or permanence.

An independent replica MUST acknowledge its own funded retention commitment before being counted. The original provider remains liable to the customer for its advertised replica count. It cannot transfer liability simply by announcing a peer. Test restores from those copies periodically. Planned closure requires no new sales, funding remaining obligations, owner notification, export and settlement. Sudden failure is handled by pre-existing client backups and independent copies; an API at a dead provider is not an exit strategy.

## Billing and reserves

Units are nontransferable prepaid service entitlements, not withdrawable wallets or yield products. No user-to-user balance transfers, exchange, or general money transmission is part of this profile. Refunds return a documented service payment under authenticated procedures, subject to applicable rights; terms cannot disclaim statutory rights. Separate provider operating funds, outstanding obligations and any customer money that applicable law requires to be segregated. Neither these labels nor a noncustodial design establish a regulatory exemption: assess the actual flow under [the compliance specification](./04-compliance.md).

Unused credits do not expire, as specified in [Funding and immutable plans](#funding-and-immutable-plans). They remain liabilities until consumed or lawfully settled, including foreseeable cost inflation. Stop selling an uneconomic plan; do not unilaterally change previously sold credits. Monthly reserve requirement is at least the SUM, over independently exercisable entitlements, of max(contractual refund exposure, stressed fulfillment cost for that entitlement), PLUS remaining costs of issued retention/read obligations, unpaid subcontractor commitments and closure/export costs. Model the worst permitted joint refund/use exercise; offset only mutually exclusive costs for the same obligation, never unrelated users or plans. Avoid counting the same underlying cost twice. For example, two independent obligations with (refund,fulfillment) exposures (100,1) and (1,100) require 200, not max(101,101). Earmarked reserves cannot also count as unrestricted operating runway. Crypto reserves are valued under a disclosed downside stress and liquidity haircut against the currency in which bills are payable. A signed balance report is not proof of solvency; independent reconciliation is required for public solvency claims.

## Capacity and reserve controls

Admission preflight checks authentication, plan ownership, available units, exact content inventory and service limits before any replication or other expensive I/O. Reserve capacity temporarily, do not debit failed work, and run at most one in-flight worker per (author_id,plan_id,message_id). Public URLs are never fetched to satisfy content inventory. Each operator publishes enforceable per-author and aggregate staging-byte, attempt-rate, concurrency, replica-fanout and cumulative-work/time budgets; offer acceptance requires those budgets to support its promised maximum. Bound work even for fully authenticated users, return 429 before excess work, release temporary reservations on a fixed deadline, and limit repeated new IDs under the same author/provider budget. Receipt retry recovery remains independent of these new-work limits. Do not rely on payment alone to prevent denial of service.

Use checked arithmetic for all byte/unit sums, durations and deadlines before reservations/debit. Reject negative, non-safe-integer or unrepresentable results. Every resulting retention, renewal and grace deadline must remain within `0001-01-01T00:00:00Z` through `9999-12-31T23:59:59Z`; overflow returns 422 `deadline_out_of_range` with no charge. Individually valid durations do not imply that their sum or calendar result is valid.

Reconcile reserves daily, and immediately after large losses, chain reorganizations, supplier failures or material price changes. A shortfall stops new plan sales, invoice issuance and renewals until covered; mark still-valid unpaid invoices as financially exposed and notify affected owners through authenticated channels. Honor legal rights and already-sold entitlements using reserved funds and an orderly recovery/exit plan, notify owners of material shortfall, and document independent reconciliation. Do not advertise solvency, fund old liabilities with undisclosed new sales, confiscate credits or silently deny purchased service to conceal the deficit. Publish aggregated status without customer financial data. Incoming transfers to old invoice addresses require the exception/refund process; they are not permission to resume sales.

## Operator-to-operator compensation

Each contract identifies payer, payee, service, period, unit price, budget cap, resource limits, independently verifiable acceptance criteria, dispute process and settlement deadline. Authorize the budget before work. Pay only reconciled accepted work within the cap, with idempotent settlement IDs. Parties disclose common ownership and related-party traffic. Self-generated requests, duplicated receipts, failed retrievals, forged byte counts and mutual traffic loops create no automatic entitlement. Discovery/retrieval is paid by explicit subscriptions or contracts, not self-reported engagement. Sponsored allowances are funded by a sponsor for a named entitlement; they create no right to inspect private reading or control ranking. Donated infrastructure is disclosed as a subsidy, not revenue from users.

## Unit economics and stress tests

Measure each role separately; do not hide an unprofitable discovery service inside ingress volume. A monthly model MUST state paying users, price net of tax/refunds, conversion and churn, actual accepted messages, retained bytes and durations, replicas, read traffic, index requests, staffing, moderation/appeals, security, legal/accounting, payment fees, taxes and subcontractors. Count monetary costs consistently in one reporting currency. Do not count prepaid future service obligations as fully earned profit. Revenue among related operators cancels in the consolidated network model.

`earned_revenue - variable_delivery_cost - fixed_operating_cost - reserve_replenishment = operating_surplus`.

For each role, show baseline and at least: 10x public reads, 5x abuse/appeal workload, 50% payer loss, 50% reserve-asset price decline, twofold storage/egress price, and loss of the largest subcontractor. Combine payer loss and cost increase. Demonstrate bounded costs through preapproved throttles/caps; mandatory legal/security duties cannot simply be throttled away. A quota-exhausted service returns an explicit 429 with retry information, offers a separately approved plan, or uses an independently funded alternate; never silently bills the author. Prevent one attacker draining a shared public-read budget using locally chosen rate limits/caching and a separately reserved owner/export budget. Return 503 when paid capacity cannot be delivered, and apply remedies; mislabeling failure as quota exhaustion is prohibited.

## Sustainability claim gate

A pilot may run at a loss if its subsidy and runway are disclosed. It MUST NOT claim self-sustaining operation until each essential role has three consecutive months of positive operating surplus after full costs and reserve replenishment, current obligations are funded, and there is at least six months of unrestricted fixed-cost runway or enforceable committed funding. These are project release criteria, not regulatory solvency standards or a guarantee. Do not fill missing metrics with invented numbers. `examples/economics-scenarios.json` is synthetic planning data only. [Governance](./06-governance.md) defines the pilot and evidence gate.
